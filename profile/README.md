We study how AI can build things from real parts: choosing components from an existing inventory and connecting them in physically valid ways.

### Recursive compositional intelligence

Our research explores how parts become wholes, and how completed assemblies can become components of larger designs.

- **Interfaces:** representing the ways parts connect.
- **Constraints:** satisfying geometry, contact, collision, support and available inventory together.
- **Reuse:** carrying what is learned from one assembly into new parts and structures.

### BricksChat

Our first application, [BricksChat](https://brickschat.com), turns ideas, sketches and photos into original brick builds with building instructions.
